A GOOD PLACE TO START

Small steps.
Useful discoveries.

Use OutsideClass for independent learning, family time or your next lesson.

Students

  1. Choose a topic you are learning, such as symmetry or plant growth.
  2. Choose a safe nearby space and a time you can spare.
  3. Read the card and safety notes before starting.
  4. Name the spot and record what you saw, your possible explanation and another possibility. Return another day to test your ideas.

Parents

  1. Choose Family mode and your children’s age range.
  2. Check the materials and agree where everyone will stay.
  3. Let each child explain what they notice.
  4. Use the discussion questions to compare discoveries.

Teachers

  1. Enter the coursework topic and choose Class mode.
  2. Set group size, ages and available lesson time.
  3. Review the generated steps and print the card.
  4. Discuss observations and record questions for the next class.

Make it work for your learners

Can’t go outside?

Choose “By a window” for observations indoors at a closed window. Choose “From a seat” to request steps without walking, standing or reaching far.

What if the AI is unavailable?

The Activities page includes three ready-made cards. After your first online visit, cached pages, sample activities and saved notebook entries can work offline in supported browsers.

How do I return to the same spot?

Give your spot a nickname on the Activities page. After you return, write what you noticed, what you think it means and another possible reason. Save the reflection, open your Notebook and choose “Return to this spot.” Check the weather again before generating the new activity. Your earlier answers are sent to Gemma for that request.

Where are my notes stored?

Only in this browser on this device. They do not sync between devices. Clearing browser data deletes them. Print a card to keep a paper copy.

Does the timer ring when my phone is locked?

The timer checks elapsed time when you return, but it does not send a background alarm. Use your phone’s alarm if you need a sound while the screen is locked.

Before a group goes outside

An adult should review the AI-generated activity, check conditions and access needs, agree boundaries and keep learners in sight. Stop if conditions are unsafe.
